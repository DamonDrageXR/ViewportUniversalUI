Place the model — iPad landscape

Target: iPad Pro · 1366×1024 · State 2 of 3 · Status: review · Updated 2026-09-17

Intent

The middle state, and the one that gets broken most often. The experience is live but held: you are lining the model up with the world, which means you must be able to see the world the whole time you are adjusting it. So the view never closes, and the UI gets one panel — not a stack of floating cards over the thing you are trying to judge.

Why it is built this way

  • Exactly one panel, edge-justified, capped at 35% of the width. One panel has one gap to misjudge; a floating card has four.
  • The panel is opaque with a heavy edge. A translucent panel over a bright site is the easiest contrast failure in the product.
  • Commit and cancel are pinned to the bottom and never scroll, because the experience is held for as long as you are in here.
  • The spatial tags are world-anchored, not interface: they do not follow the viewer, they need not be reachable, and the far one fades with distance.

Open questions

  • Should the panel be dismissible to a handle while still held, or does hiding the controls invite leaving the model half-placed?
  • Nudge in metres or in the model's own units? Shown in metres.
