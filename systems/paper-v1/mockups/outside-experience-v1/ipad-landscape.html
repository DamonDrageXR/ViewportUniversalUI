Session browser — iPad landscape

Target: iPad Pro · 1366×1024 · State 1 of 3 · Status: review · Updated 2026-09-17

Intent

The same first state on the tablet the crew actually carries. A full navigation rail is allowed because there is no spatial view to protect yet — this is the screen where you choose what to open, and it disappears entirely once an experience is running.

Why it is built this way

  • Two columns rather than a phone list: at 1366 pt wide a single column wastes the screen and pushes the pick below the fold.
  • Every session card is a whole target, not a title link. Gloved hands do not hit 13 pt text.
  • The current rail item is filled black. Selection is never a colour here, so it cannot be mistaken for a status.
  • Counts and durations are in the numeral face so they line up down the column.

Open questions

  • Does the thumbnail earn its space before a session has ever been opened, or should the first card be a large "join by code" tile?
  • Archived sessions: a rail item, or a filter on this list?
