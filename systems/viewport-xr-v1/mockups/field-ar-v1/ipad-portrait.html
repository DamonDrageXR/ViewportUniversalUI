Site brief — iPad portrait

Target: iPad Pro · portrait · 1024×1366 · Status: draft · Updated 2026-09-17

Intent

The consultation view — an iPad held in two hands and turned toward someone else. Reads like a document rather than a tool: what is proposed, where, and what it affects.

Why it is built this way

  • Portrait stacks scene over detail. Landscape flanks the scene with panels; the same layout rotated would waste the width.
  • No tool rail — this view is for showing, not editing. Editing lives in the landscape layout.
  • The legend sits over the scene rather than beside it, so the render stays the full width of the device.

Open questions

  • Should the lower half be swipeable between Layers, Heritage and Measurements, rather than a fixed split?
  • Does the stat block belong here, or is it detail that only matters in the office?
