Field capture — phone

Target: Phone · 393×852 · Status: draft · Updated 2026-09-17

Intent

What the app looks like on a phone — the device people actually have on them when they are not carrying the iPad. Scoped down to: see where you are, toggle what is shown, capture a note or photo.

Why it is built this way

  • Every control is in the bottom third, because the top of a 6.1" screen is out of thumb reach one-handed.
  • Layer toggles are horizontally scrolling chips rather than a tree — a tree needs two hands.
  • The capture button is --hit-xr, the largest target in the system, because it is pressed in gloves without looking.

Open questions

  • Is capture a photo, a marker, or a chooser? Shown here as one button with a long-press menu.
  • Should the sheet be draggable to full height for the layer tree, or is the chip row enough?
